01 · Delivery

Shipping

We deliver across Pakistan. Orders are typically delivered within 3–5 working days after confirmation; delivery to remote areas may take longer. Our team may contact you on WhatsApp or by phone to confirm your Cash on Delivery order.

Standard delivery is PKR 250. Delivery is free on orders of PKR 5,000 or more. The applicable delivery charge is shown in your order summary before checkout.

Please provide a complete address and a reachable Pakistani mobile number. Once your order is dispatched, keep your phone available for the courier.

02 · The next chapter

Returns & exchange

Thriftora pieces are individually sourced and one of a kind, so we encourage you to review all photos, condition notes and measurements before ordering. For a wrong, damaged or materially misdescribed item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order reference and clear photos so we can help find a fair solution.

Change-of-mind returns and size exchanges are not guaranteed because a replacement in the same piece may not exist. Please message us before sending anything back; unapproved returns may not be accepted. Any agreed return must be unused and in the condition received.

For help, visit Contact or message us via WhatsApp.

03 · Using our shop

Terms

By browsing or ordering from Thriftora, you agree to use this site lawfully and provide accurate order information. Product descriptions, measurements and condition notes are provided in good faith; slight colour variation may occur between screens.

Each item is a pre-loved individual piece and availability is limited to the stock shown. We may correct listing errors, confirm orders before dispatch, or cancel an order if a piece is no longer available. Prices are listed in Pakistani Rupees and delivery charges are shown at checkout.

04 · Your information

Privacy

We use the details you provide to respond to enquiries, prepare and deliver orders, and provide customer support. When you place an order, the checkout opens WhatsApp with a message addressed to our configured business number. You choose whether to send it; WhatsApp processes messages under its own privacy terms.

This static demo stores cart and wishlist data in your browser’s local storage. The newsletter form confirms locally and does not transmit or store an email address. No payment card information is collected on this site.

You can clear saved cart and wishlist data by clearing this site’s local storage in your browser. For privacy questions, please contact us.

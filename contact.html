FAQs.

All policies ↗
Are your pieces new?

They’re pre-loved, one-of-a-kind finds. We inspect every piece and clearly label its condition before it goes on the site.

How do I choose the right size?

Each product page includes available sizes and garment measurements. Compare them with a similar piece you own, or message us for help.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is available across Pakistan. Shipping charges are shown in your cart before you place your order.

How long will delivery take?

Most orders arrive within 3–5 working days after confirmation. Remote areas may take a little longer. Visit our shipping policy for details.

Can I return or exchange something?

As every thrift piece is unique, we ask you to check photos and measurements before ordering. See our returns and exchange policy.

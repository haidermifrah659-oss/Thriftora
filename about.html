Our story

Found with care.
Passed with love.

We started Thriftora with a simple feeling: there is already so much good clothing in the world. The denim that fits just right. The jacket with the perfect fade. The dress that feels like it was waiting for you.

So we began curating pre-loved pieces for people who like their style with a little character. Every Thriftora find is checked, described honestly and given room for its next chapter.

From rail to you

Our little process.

We source with an eye for quality, then inspect each item, note the condition and take measurements to help you choose with confidence. Once you find the one, we pack it up and send it your way across Pakistan.
